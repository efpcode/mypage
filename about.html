About Me

From DNA to Coding Away

I discovered the joys of coding during my bachelor's degree in Molecular Biology. Like many others, my first introduction to this world was through the programming language Python (version 2.6).

My iterative mindset fuels my continuous drive for improvement and that has lead me to everlasting journey that is testing and coding.

I was immediately hooked on how fast and direct the feedback loop was, especially in contrast to world of biology, where experiments could take a month or more to yield a meaningful result.
